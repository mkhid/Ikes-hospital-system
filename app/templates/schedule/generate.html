Any date in the target week is accepted; the roster always runs Monday to Sunday.
Regenerating replaces an existing draft for the same week. A published roster must be withdrawn first, so staff are never silently moved.

How the engine builds your roster

A two-phase hybrid: feasibility first, fairness second.

Phase 1 · Feasible construction

The week is walked in calendar order, night shifts first, because each decision constrains the next. A staff member is only offered a slot if they pass every hard constraint. Nothing illegal is ever produced and then repaired.

  • One shift per person per day
  • Minimum rest between shifts
  • Maximum weekly hours
  • Maximum consecutive days and nights
  • Approved leave and night-duty clearance
  • Department minimum staffing per shift

Phase 2 · Heuristic optimisation

The feasible roster is then improved by hill climbing over three moves: fill a gap, reassign a slot, swap two slots. Every candidate move is re-checked against the same hard constraints, so feasibility holds at every step of the search.

  • Even spread of night shifts
  • Even spread of weekend duty
  • Even total hours
  • Shift preferences where possible
  • Recent weeks carried forward, so fairness rolls over

Where no legal assignment exists the slot is recorded as a coverage gap and escalated, rather than filled by breaking a rule. A gap is a decision for a human, not a failure of the engine.
