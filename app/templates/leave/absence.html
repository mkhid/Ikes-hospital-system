Absence details

This takes effect immediately and does not wait for approval. The person's shifts on that date are released and the engine assigns replacements straight away. Anything it cannot cover is escalated to you as a coverage gap.

What happens next

The sequence the system runs the moment you submit.

  • Step 1 Shifts released. Every shift the staff member holds on that date is marked vacated. The original assignment is kept, not deleted, so the roster shows who was meant to work as well as who actually covers.
  • Step 2 Replacements found. For each vacated shift the engine ranks colleagues who pass every hard constraint and picks the one carrying the lightest share of that kind of duty.
  • Step 3 People told. Each replacement receives an in-app message, an email and an SMS naming the shift and the reason.
  • Step 4 Gaps escalated. Any shift with no compliant candidate is flagged on the roster and sent to you and HR, rather than filled by breaking a rest rule.
  • Step 5 Recorded. Every step is written to the hash-chained audit trail with your name and the time against it.
